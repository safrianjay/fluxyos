A few things to know.

What gets read, what you review, and where the receipt goes.

How does Receipt Capture work?

Upload a receipt photo or PDF in the scan workflow. Fluxy AI reads the merchant, date, total, currency, and a suggested category. Review and correct the details, choose how the expense affects cash, and save. The original receipt stays attached to the transaction.

Which receipt files can I upload?

Scanning accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, and PDF files up to 10 MB, one document at a time. Attachments have a 5 MB limit: large images are compressed where possible, but keep PDFs under 5 MB to retain the source with the expense. Use a clear photo or digital copy in the web workflow.

Does AI extract every item, tax, and payment detail?

Not as separate structured fields today. The scanner extracts key document details and a suggested category. Item lines, tax breakdowns, and payment-method details remain on the original receipt for you to check. Confirm tax treatment and the cash-impact setting before saving.

Can I correct the AI result before saving?

Yes. Edit the merchant, amount, date, currency, category, and accounting account in the review step. Uncertain fields can show confidence indicators and warnings. A scan does not create a financial record until you save it.

Which currencies does receipt scanning recognize?

The scanner recognizes IDR, USD, and SGD. Review the detected currency and exchange rate. Expense transactions are saved in the workspace base currency; the original amount and conversion details are kept in notes. Supplier bills follow a separate original-currency workflow.

Where can I find a saved receipt?

Find the expense in Transactions using merchant or category search and the date filter, then open its attached document. Receipt Capture does not currently have a separate receipt-history page or a guaranteed retention period.

Can I submit receipts through WhatsApp or email?

Receipt scanning currently starts with a document upload in FluxyOS. A dedicated WhatsApp receipt bot and inbound receipt-email inbox are not available today. You can upload a receipt file you have received through either channel.
