A few things to know.

What can I track with Dynamic Budgeting? +

Create an annual plan, divide it into budget periods, and allocate amounts to spending categories. Track assigned expenses against those allocations.

How does actual spend update? +

Actual spend comes from expenses assigned to a budget allocation. You can review the linked transactions to understand what makes up each total.

Can I adjust my budget? +

Yes. Review and update your plans, periods, and allocations as your spending priorities change.

Does a budget stop a payment? +

No. Dynamic Budgeting gives you visibility into planned and actual spend. It is not a payment approval or spending-blocking system.

How does Fluxy AI help? +

Ask questions about your financial data to understand expenses and business performance. Review the underlying records before making a spending decision.
