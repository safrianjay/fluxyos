Clear on what
happens automatically.

The connection, the controls, and the boundaries.

What does Accounting Automation actually automate?+

Supported source workflows create balanced journals using posting rules and account mappings. Posted journals update ledger balances, which feed the Accounting Center’s financial statements. Review, mapping, and open-period checks still apply; not every transaction type has an automatic posting rule.

Which activities connect to the Accounting Center?+

Supported sources include POS sales and refunds, issued invoices and their recorded payments, bills and their payments, goods receipts, configured stock-cost movements, and saved expenses. POS and inventory availability depend on workspace eligibility and setup.

Do foreign-currency documents post automatically?+

No. Each workspace keeps one fixed base currency: IDR, PHP, SGD, or MYR. Invoices and bills can retain supported document currencies, but foreign-document flows are excluded from automatic journal posting. Review their accounting treatment separately. There is no display-currency translation or multi-entity consolidation.

Does Fluxy AI decide the accounting for me?+

Fluxy AI extracts supported document fields, suggests categories, and answers questions about recorded financial data. You review document results before saving. Posting rules—not an autonomous AI agent—determine journal lines. Automatic anomaly detection and forecasting are not part of this feature.

What happens when a record cannot be posted?+

The Accounting Center surfaces unposted sources and mapping or period issues. Review and resolve the cause before posting. Ledger-derived statements include posted journal balances, not every saved operational record. Period-close checks help you identify missing postings before closing the books.

Can I trace a report number back to its source?+

Yes. Accounting Center reports connect account lines to the General Ledger, journal detail, and source records. Journal references preserve the link to the operational activity, so finance teams can check the underlying record instead of re-entering it.
