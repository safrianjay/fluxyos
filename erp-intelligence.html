What is FluxyOS ERP Intelligence?+

FluxyOS ERP Intelligence connects accounting, financial reporting, finance operations, and Fluxy AI around the same underlying finance records.

Does FluxyOS replace an ERP?+

No. FluxyOS is an Intelligent Finance Operating System, not an ERP replacement. It brings ERP-class finance depth and intelligence to the connected finance workflow.

What can Fluxy AI help a team understand?+

Fluxy AI can help users ask questions about revenue, expenses, cash movement, transactions, budgets, and the records behind a reported number.

Which financial reports are available?+

FluxyOS includes an Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow Statement, trial balance, General Ledger, and supporting finance reports.
