Frequently asked questions

What are FluxyOS AI Agents?+

Fluxy AI brings finance Q&A and document extraction into FluxyOS. It helps you work with recorded financial data, review extracted details, and understand business performance.

Can Fluxy AI move money or run finance on its own?+

No. Fluxy AI is not an autonomous finance team. Use it to understand information and review document details; your team remains responsible for decisions and saved records.

What financial questions can I ask?+

Ask about recorded revenue, expenses, cash positions, transactions, and financial performance. The answer depends on the data available in your workspace.

Do I need to review extracted documents?+

Yes. Check amounts, dates, vendors, and categories before saving. AI can make mistakes, and a clear source record makes accounting easier to verify.

Does Fluxy AI replace financial reports?+

No. Financial statements and ledger records remain the source for accounting. Fluxy AI helps explain the information; verify important answers against the relevant records.

Can I use Bahasa Indonesia?+

Yes. Fluxy AI supports financial questions in Bahasa Indonesia and English. Keep business names, amounts, and time periods clear in your question.
