Questions, answered

Find the right fit
for your financial work.

Practical details before your team gets started.

How does FluxyOS help a manufacturing finance team?+

FluxyOS connects customer invoices, supplier bills, recorded spending, budgets, cash records, and accounting. Your team can review collections, upcoming payments, and spending allocations together. The quality of the view depends on the records you enter and post.

Does it calculate production costs by batch?+

Manufacturing BOMs, production runs, work in progress, yield, and automatic batch costing are not currently available. Record supported bills and expenses for financial review; do not treat them as an automatically calculated production cost. Discuss your manufacturing requirements with sales.

Is a remaining budget the same as available cash?+

No. A budget allocation is a spending plan. Recorded cash reflects the cash records in your workspace, while unpaid bills and invoices show separate commitments. Review these together and keep payment records current.

What should we prepare to get started?+

Prepare your customer invoices, supplier bills, opening cash records, chart of accounts, and spending categories. Start with one workspace and review the setup with your finance team. Sales can help you assess fit before choosing a plan.

Can it consolidate multiple manufacturing companies?+

Multi-entity consolidation and per-factory production profitability are not currently supported. This walkthrough concerns financial records within one workspace. Confirm your company structure and reporting needs with sales.
