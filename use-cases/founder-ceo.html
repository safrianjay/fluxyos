Questions, answered

A practical starting point
for founders and CEOs.

What can I review before a new commitment?+

Review recorded cash, unpaid bills and their due dates, subscriptions, and spending assigned to budgets. These views depend on the records in your workspace. Keep them current before making a spending decision.

Is this a cash-flow forecast?+

No. Recorded cash and commitments help you review the current position; predictive cash-flow forecasting is not currently available. Due dates show recorded obligations, not a guarantee of future cash availability.

Can I trace a financial result to its records?+

The Accounting Center provides posted statements, the trial balance, ledger, and journal detail. Check the period and accounting basis, then inspect the source entries with your finance team.

What can Fluxy AI help explain?+

Fluxy AI answers financial questions using the data recorded in your workspace. Review its answer against the underlying records. It does not approve spending, execute payments, or replace your financial judgment.

Can I use this for separate companies or investor reporting?+

Multi-entity consolidation and a dedicated investor dashboard are not currently available. Financial report previews, browser print/save as PDF, and the accounting CSV package can support a review. Check completeness and your reporting requirements before sharing.
