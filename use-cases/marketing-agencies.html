A few things to know

Clear answers.
Real expectations.

What is available, what needs your review, and what is still ahead.

What can FluxyOS help an agency manage today?+

Create client invoices, track receivables, record vendor bills and expenses, attach receipts, review budgets and cash balances, and prepare business financial reports. These are general business workflows that an agency can use.

Can I see automated profitability by client or project?+

No. Automated client or project profitability is not available. You can record project-related expenses with descriptions and categories, but financial statements on this page are at business level. Project attribution remains product direction.

Does FluxyOS connect to our advertising platforms?+

This page does not offer live integrations with agency advertising or project tools. Record those costs manually or use supported CSV imports. An integration logo or a planned connector is not evidence of live synchronisation.

Does it pay vendors or approve spending automatically?+

No. Bill records, due dates, and payment status help organise your payables; they do not move money. Approval workflows are planned. AI document extraction requires your review, and Fluxy AI answers depend on the recorded data.

Does FluxyOS support multiple entities or predictive forecasts?+

Multi-entity accounting and predictive cash-flow forecasting are not available. Cash visibility here means recorded balances and financial records, not an AI prediction. See the current plans or discuss your requirements with sales.
