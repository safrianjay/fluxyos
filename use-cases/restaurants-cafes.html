Questions, answered

Before you connect
your restaurant.

A few practical details about the financial connections.

How do restaurant sales connect to accounting?+

Paid FluxyOS POS orders create revenue transactions with source references. Eligible accounting posting creates journals; financial statements reflect posted entries. Finance can review records that still need posting or reconciliation.

Does every menu sale include an ingredient cost?+

Ingredient consumption requires configured items and recipes. Cost-of-sales entries use recorded inventory costs. Missing mappings or costs need review; an order can be sold without complete ingredient costing.

Can I compare financial performance by outlet?+

Outlet profit-and-loss reporting uses posted ledger entries tagged to configured outlet dimensions and depends on access and correct attribution. Untagged entries remain unassigned. This is not multi-entity consolidation or an all-outlet POS dashboard.

Will FluxyOS decide whether I should buy equipment or expand?+

No. Recorded cash, costs, unpaid bills, and budgets provide context for your review. These planning examples do not forecast returns, provide financing or investment advice, or automatically allocate money.

Where can I explore table orders and POS?+

Visit the Point of Sale page for tables, orders, payment recording, receipts, and related operating workflows. This page focuses on how restaurant activity connects to the financial system.
