Questions, answered

A practical starting point
for department heads.

How can I review spending for a department?+

Use budget allocations to organise the spending you want to review. Assigned transactions and bills provide the underlying records. A department name can describe an allocation; it does not create a separate department dashboard.

Does remaining budget mean cash is available?+

No. Remaining allocation compares assigned spending with the plan. Review recorded cash, unpaid bills, and due dates with finance before making a new commitment.

Can FluxyOS approve or block department spending?+

Budget approval workflows and hard spending enforcement are not currently available. Use allocation records to support your team’s existing review and approval process.

Who can access the financial records?+

Access follows the roles and permissions in your workspace. Work with the workspace owner and finance team to choose the appropriate access. Allocations do not provide a separate department-level permission boundary.
