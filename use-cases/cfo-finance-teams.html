Questions, answered

A practical starting point
for your finance team.

Can FluxyOS support month-end close?+

Yes. The Accounting Center has a close-readiness review, trial balance, posted journals, and period close. An authorized user closes the period after resolving blocking unposted sources and an unbalanced trial balance. Supporting-document completeness remains part of your team’s review.

Do financial statements use posted accounting entries?+

The posted income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement use ledger balances. Check the basis and period of the view you open: records-based operational previews and posted accounting statements serve different reviews.

Does bank reconciliation include live bank feeds?+

The verified workflow imports bank statements and supports match review and reconciliation. This page does not promise a live bank feed. Keep statement imports and payment records current for an accurate comparison.

Can we consolidate separate companies or approve payments?+

Separate-entity consolidation and approval workflows are not currently available. FluxyOS connects the financial records within a workspace; it does not move money or execute supplier payments. Discuss your reporting structure and required controls with sales.

What can we share with an accountant?+

The accounting export package includes CSV files for the income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance, and general ledger, with period and tie-out context. Reports & Exports also supports financial report previews and browser print/save as PDF. Review the report basis and completeness before sharing.
