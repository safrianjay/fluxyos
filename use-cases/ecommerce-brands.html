What is FluxyOS for e-commerce brands?+

FluxyOS is an Intelligent Finance Operating System. It connects supported commerce data with stock costing, invoices, expenses, accounting, financial reporting, and Fluxy AI in one workspace. It does not replace your storefront or fulfilment system.

Which marketplaces can I connect?+

FluxyOS has connectors for TikTok Shop and Shopee. Connection and sync depend on platform authorisation and configuration. Other financial records can be added manually or imported through supported CSV workflows; other storefronts are not advertised as live integrations.

How do orders connect to inventory and accounting?+

For stock-holding workspaces, imported marketplace orders can be matched to item SKUs. The stock-cost posting action records stock movements and balanced cost-of-goods-sold journals using recorded inventory costs. Unmatched or uncosted items need review. FluxyOS does not push stock availability back to marketplaces.

Can I manage customer and supplier invoices?+

Yes. Create and email customer invoices, track receivables, and record supplier bills and payment status. AI can extract uploaded invoice and receipt data for your review before recording. Supported document currencies are kept alongside their accounting values.

What can Fluxy AI tell me?+

Ask about recorded revenue, expenses, cash flow, and financial performance. Fluxy AI uses available workspace financial data and context. Answers depend on data coverage; it is not a promise of autonomous purchasing, product-level predictions, or inventory forecasting.

Does FluxyOS forecast sales or stock demand?+

No. This page demonstrates recorded financial performance and budget-versus-actual tracking, not predictive sales or inventory forecasts. Complete your source records and account mappings to get meaningful financial statements.
