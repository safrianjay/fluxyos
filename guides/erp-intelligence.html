Finance guide

What is ERP intelligence?

ERP intelligence connects clean, categorized finance records to an existing ERP. It helps a team retain its established ERP while improving the operational context and quality of finance data that reaches it.

What it is—and is not

ERP intelligence is not a claim that one tool replaces every ERP workflow. FluxyOS is an Intelligent Finance Operating System, not an ERP replacement. The value is a connected finance operation that can send reviewed, categorized journal entries into an existing ERP workflow.

How the workflow fits together

  • Operational activity creates the finance context.
  • FluxyOS helps organize records, receipts, budgets, and reconciliation exceptions.
  • Teams review the exceptions and use clean records in their established ERP process.

Questions, answered

Does FluxyOS replace an ERP?

No. FluxyOS can operate alongside an existing ERP and is not marketed as an ERP replacement.

What records can be sent to an ERP?

FluxyOS is designed to push clean, categorized journal entries into an existing ERP workflow. Confirm the implementation details with the team for your environment.
