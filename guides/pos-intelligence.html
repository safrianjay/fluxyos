Finance guide

What is POS intelligence?

POS intelligence is the connection between operational sales activity and financial records. It brings orders, recorded payments, and configured item costs together so a restaurant or café team can review revenue and costs with the context behind them.

What it connects

  • Table and QR-menu orders
  • Recorded cash, card, and QRIS payments
  • Configured items, recipes, and cost information
  • The accounting records used for financial reporting

Why the connection matters

A payment total alone cannot explain which order created it, whether the right item cost was configured, or what needs review. Connecting the operational event to its finance record helps teams investigate exceptions before month-end rather than rebuilding the story in a spreadsheet.

What FluxyOS does today

FluxyOS Point of Sale supports table service, reservations, orders, QR ordering, manual payment recording, and shifts for eligible workspaces. Sales connect to accounting and configured recipes can contribute cost information. Payment-provider processing and offline queues are not available yet.

Questions, answered

Does POS intelligence replace accounting?

No. It adds operational context. Your accounting records remain the source of truth.

Does FluxyOS process card or QRIS payments?

Staff can record cash, card, and QRIS payments. Integrated payment-provider processing is not available yet.
