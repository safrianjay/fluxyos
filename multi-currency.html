What works today, and how your books stay consistent.

Which currencies can I use in FluxyOS?+

Workspace base currencies are IDR, PHP, SGD, and MYR. Invoices and bills support IDR, USD, SGD, PHP, and MYR as document currencies. USD is a document currency, not a workspace base currency today.

Can I change my workspace currency later?+

No. Choose the base currency during onboarding. It stays fixed so transactions, journals, budgets, and reports use the same accounting basis. The market selectors on this page show separate example workspaces; they do not change your books.

Can POS accept any currency at checkout?+

POS uses the workspace base currency for prices, sales, payments, and accounting. It is not a foreign-currency tender or currency-conversion service. POS availability depends on your workspace eligibility.

Do foreign-currency invoices automatically post to the General Ledger?+

Not today. Foreign-currency invoices and bills retain their original currency. Their payments can record an exchange rate and a base-currency transaction, but those foreign-document flows are excluded from automatic journal posting. Review the accounting treatment separately.

How does Receipt Capture handle a foreign receipt?+

AI currently recognizes IDR, USD, and SGD. Review the currency, amount, category, and exchange rate before saving an expense. The expense uses the workspace base currency, with the original amount and conversion details in notes. Supplier bills use a separate original-currency flow.

Are budgets and financial reports multi-currency?+

Budgets, dashboard KPIs, and financial statements use the workspace base currency. They do not mix raw amounts from different currencies or offer a display-currency switch. Account for foreign activity before relying on the posted-ledger reports.

Can FluxyOS consolidate companies or markets into one report?+

Not today. Each workspace keeps its own books and reporting currency. Multi-entity consolidation, cross-workspace currency translation, and automatic FX revaluation are not part of this feature.
