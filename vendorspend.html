A few things
worth knowing.

From your first draft to your books.

Can I create and email invoices in FluxyOS?

Yes. Add customer details, line items, payment terms, and tax. Review the invoice and choose “Finalize and send.” With invoice email delivery enabled, FluxyOS generates the PDF and emails it to your customer automatically. Track delivery and resend from the invoice.

What does AI invoice scanning extract?

The scanner reads supplier details, invoice number, invoice date, due date, currency, and total, and suggests a category. Review and correct the fields before saving a bill. Structured line-item, tax, and bank-detail extraction is not currently included.

Can I receive supplier invoices here?

You can upload a supplier invoice as a PDF or supported image, review the extracted details, and save a bill in FluxyOS. This is an upload-and-review workflow, not a dedicated inbound email inbox.

Which invoice statuses can I track?

Track draft, open, partially paid, overdue, paid, and void invoices. Email delivery is tracked separately. A sent email does not mean the customer has viewed or paid the invoice.

Which currencies are supported?

Create invoices in IDR, USD, SGD, PHP, and MYR. AI bill scanning currently recognizes IDR, USD, and SGD. Foreign-currency invoices retain their original amounts and are not automatically consolidated into the ledger.

How do invoices connect to accounting?

Supported base-currency invoice workflows create journal entries for issuance and payment, connecting receivables and revenue to the ledger and financial reports. Supplier bills connect expenses and payables through their own accounting workflow. Foreign-currency records have separate accounting limitations.
